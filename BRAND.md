# ARC//ONE Brand Profile

## Essence

ARC//ONE is a fictional elite esports arena and performance system. Its voice is precise, competitive, cinematic, and grounded in live-event tension.

**Three-word feeling:** engineered, electric, decisive.

## Aesthetic direction

Retro-futuristic industrial esports. The design should feel like an arena broadcast package: graphite surfaces, acid-lime signal accents, technical rules, equipment-scale imagery, and measured movement.

## Color strategy

- Ink/background: `#070906`
- Surface: `#0e120d`
- Acid signal: `#d7ff3f`
- Cream text: `#f4f7ef`
- Steel/muted text: `#899084`
- Signal red: `#ff5d47`

Use acid-lime for attention and status, not as a large all-purpose background. Preserve strong readable contrast.

## Typography

- UI and body: the project's existing Inter variable font.
- Technical labels and data: the project's existing Space Mono variable font.
- Titles: condensed, uppercase, tightly tracked, and high-impact through scale and composition rather than gradients.

## Layout and motion

- Use 4px-based spacing with generous dramatic gaps at section boundaries.
- Favor editorial asymmetry, borders, rails, and controlled information density over symmetric card grids.
- The key interaction is scroll-led storytelling. Keep motion purposeful, performant, and disabled or simplified for `prefers-reduced-motion`.

## Do not introduce without an explicit request

- Purple/pink dark-mode gradients, generic 3D blobs, or AI-brain imagery.
- Generic SaaS hero formulas, floating product mockups, testimonial carousels, or arbitrary animated counters.
- A new type family that competes with the established system.
