# ARC//ONE Design Brief

## Goal

Make the ARC//ONE esports arena feel like a world-final broadcast experience while guiding visitors from stadium atmosphere to competition hardware and event CTA.

## Audience

Competitive players, esports viewers, event attendees, and performance-hardware enthusiasts.

## Aesthetic profile

- **Feeling:** engineered, electric, decisive
- **Direction:** retro-futuristic industrial esports
- **Hierarchy:** cinematic arena imagery first; then motion-led narrative, performance data, and event action
- **Spacing:** dramatic between major chapters; compact for telemetry and specification details
- **Motion:** scroll-progress storytelling, minimal HUD-like transitions, no decorative animation noise

## Guardrails

Avoid generic SaaS composition, purple-gradient dark mode, abstract blobs, stock testimonials, and visual elements that do not support arena tension or hardware precision.
